# Lesson 05 — Generative AI and Foundation Models

Objectives:

- Explain the autoencoder objective and how the latent bottleneck enables compression.
- Describe the GAN objective and identify mode collapse.
- Explain the VAE reparameterization trick.
- Describe the denoising diffusion forward process and the reverse denoising objective.
- Define a foundation model and name three examples.
- Describe supervised fine-tuning and RLHF.

## Main Concepts

- Autoencoders: encoder-decoder structure and latent space representation
- Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs): generator-discriminator training dynamics
- Diffusion models: forward noising process and denoising score matching
- Large Language Models (LLMs): pre-training objectives, emergent capabilities, and in-context learning
- Agentic workflows: tool use, planning, and multi-step reasoning

## Learning Outcomes

- Student can describe the training objective and failure modes (mode collapse, training instability) of GANs.
- Student can compare diffusion models and GANs on sample quality vs. training stability.
- Student can fine-tune a Hugging Face Transformer checkpoint on a new text classification task and report evaluation metrics.
