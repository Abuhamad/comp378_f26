# Lesson 35 — AI Governance, Compliance, and Risk Frameworks

Objectives:

- Name the four NIST AI RMF core functions.
- Classify a given AI application under the correct EU AI Act risk tier.
- Conduct algorithmic audit covering accuracy, fairness, transparency, and accountability.
- Produce a complete AI Impact Assessment document.
- Describe at least one regulatory enforcement mechanism under the EU AI Act.

## Main Concepts

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF): Govern, Map, Measure, Manage functions
- EU AI Act: prohibited, high-risk, limited-risk, and minimal-risk classification tiers
- Algorithmic auditing: structured assessment of model behavior against compliance criteria
- AI Impact Assessment (AIIA): risk identification, affected populations, mitigation plan

## Learning Outcomes

- Student can classify a given AI application under the EU AI Act risk tier and justify the classification.
- Student can conduct a structured algorithmic audit covering accuracy, fairness, transparency, and accountability dimensions.
- Student can produce a complete AI Impact Assessment document for a high-risk AI deployment scenario.
